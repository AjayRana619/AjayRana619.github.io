It is fast, silent, and on-time to the minute. It costs ¥13,000–¥14,000 one-way. > **💡 Honest JR Pass tip:** A 7-day JR Pass costs ¥80,000. For a Tokyo-Kyoto-Osaka route, your individual Shinkansen tickets will cost roughly ¥27,000–¥30,000 return. **The pass is NOT worth it** for this specific itinerary unless you add Hiroshima, Hakone, or other destinations. Buy individual tickets via the JR West website. --- ## ⛩️ KYOTO — Days 5–7 ![Fushimi Inari Shrine torii gates in Kyoto](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1478436127897-769e1b3f0f36?w=1200&auto=format&fit=crop) Kyoto is the antidote to Tokyo. It's also Japan's most casually friendly city; people here will actually talk to you without being asked. ### Day 9 — Dotonbori & Street Food Rampage - 🐙 **Takoyaki** (octopus balls) at Dotonbori: ¥500–¥700 for 6 pieces - 🥞 **Okonomiyaki** (savory pancake): ¥800–¥1,200 - 🏯 **Osaka Castle** & park — ¥600 (castle interior) / Park is free - 🌆 Walk **Shinsaibashi** shopping street - 🌃 **Dotonbori** at night — the neon, the Glico Man, the noise — free and unmissable > **💡 Honest tip:** The best takoyaki in Osaka is not from the giant flashy stalls in Dotonbori. Carry ¥10,000–¥20,000 at all times. - **7-Eleven, Lawson, and FamilyMart ATMs** reliably accept international cards. Use these, not bank ATMs. - **Convenience store food is genuinely good.** A FamilyMart onigiri (¥150) and hot coffee (¥150) is a legitimate breakfast that won't slow you down. ### Transport - **IC cards (Suica/ICOCA) work everywhere** — trains, buses, convenience stores, vending machines. Load money, tap, go. - **Don't buy the JR Pass** unless you're traveling beyond Tokyo-Kyoto-Osaka. For this specific route, individual tickets are cheaper. - **Google Maps works flawlessly** for Japanese transit directions. Trust it completely. ### Crowds & Timing - **Arrive at famous spots at opening time** — typically 8:00 or 9:00 AM. The difference between 8:30 AM and 10:30 AM at Fushimi Inari or the Bamboo Grove is *thousands of people*. - **Avoid Golden Week** (late April–early May) and Cherry Blossom season (late March–mid April) unless you book 6–12 months in advance and are prepared for prices 30–50% higher. ### Food - **Lunch sets (*teishoku*) are the secret weapon** — restaurants that charge ¥2,500 for dinner often offer the same quality for ¥1,000–¥1,500 at lunch. - **Conveyor belt sushi (*kaiten-zushi*)** is not a tourist trap — it's legitimately good and cheap. Chains like Sushiro and Kura Sushi charge ¥110–¥165 per plate. - **Vending machines everywhere.** Hot drinks for ¥150 in winter. Cold drinks for ¥130 in summer. ### Etiquette (Abridged) - 🤫 Don't talk on the phone on trains - 🚶 Walk on the left; stand on the escalator's left side (except Osaka — they stand right, confusingly) - 🗑️ There are almost no public bins. You carry your trash until you find a convenience store bin. - 🙏 A small bow of acknowledgment goes a long way everywhere --- ## 🗓️ Day-by-Day Summary | Day | Location | Highlights | Est.
